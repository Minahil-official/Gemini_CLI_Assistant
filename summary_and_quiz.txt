**Summary:**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines, enabling them to learn, reason, and perform tasks that typically require human intellect. Key subfields include machine learning, where systems improve from experience; deep learning, which uses neural networks to mimic the human brain; natural language processing (NLP) for understanding human language; and computer vision. AI is widely applied in healthcare for diagnostics, finance for fraud detection, and transportation for self-driving cars. Despite its benefits, AI presents challenges such as ethical concerns, potential job displacement, and biases in its decision-making processes.
